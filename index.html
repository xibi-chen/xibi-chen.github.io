Xibi Chen

Assistant Professor

I am a newly joined tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), The University of Hong Kong (HKU). Strongly supported by integrated circuits & electromagnetics, my group intends to build scalable hardware systems that inspires next-generation information science and technologies. Our research aims to address emerging demands in future data centers, physical AI, power grids, biomedical engineering, and any other domains. Our research areas will broadly cover (but not be limited to) high-frequency integrated circuits & systems (e.g. microwave, mmWave, sub-THz ICs), electromagnetic theories & engineering (e.g. EM fields, computational EM, phased arrays, metasurfaces, RIS etc.), advanced packaging & system integration (e.g. system-in-package, high-speed interconnects, chiplets, heterogeneous integration etc.), wireless sensing & communications (e.g. radars, imaging, wireless links, MIMO, ISAC etc.), and multi-physical systems (e.g. thermal, mechanics, photonics etc.). If you are interested in end-to-end hardware system designs, balancing theories and practice in real-world engineering, or joining our group for any other reasons, you are welcome to apply and reach out.

Principal Investigator

Xibi Chen is currently a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Hong Kong (HKU-ECE). He received his Ph.D. degree from Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), in 2026, and his B.S. and M.S. degrees from Tsinghua University, in 2017 and 2020, respectively. From 2015 to 2017, he was a Research Assistant with the Microwave and Antenna Institute, Department of Electronic Engineering, Tsinghua University. He later became a Graduate Student Researcher in the same institute from 2017 to 2020. From 2020 to 2026, he was a Graduate Student Researcher at EECS, MIT. His research background includes terahertz (THz) integrated circuits and systems, electromagnetics, advanced packaging technologies, large-scale phased arrays, radar sensing, and high-speed communications. He worked in Texas Instruments (Kilby Lab) and Intel Corporation as Summer Research Interns in 2023 and 2024, respectively. Xibi was the recipient of 2025–2026 IEEE SSCS Predoctoral Achievement Award, and 2024 IEEE MTT-S Tom Brazil Graduate Fellowship. He also received 2025 MIT EECS MathWorks Fellowship Award, ISSCC 2022 Student Travel Grant Award, and Analog Devices Outstanding Student Designer Award.
